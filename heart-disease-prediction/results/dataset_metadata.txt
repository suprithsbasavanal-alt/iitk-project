8. thalach (Integer) - Maximum heart rate achieved

11. slope (Categorical) - Slope of peak exercise ST segment (1 = upsloping, 2 = flat, 3 = downsloping)
12. ca (Float/Integer) - Number of major vessels (0-3) colored by fluoroscopy
