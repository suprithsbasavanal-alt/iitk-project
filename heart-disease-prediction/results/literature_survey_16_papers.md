## 4. Key Takeaways & Project Alignment

1. **Benchmark Validation**: The literature validates our choice of the **UCI Cleveland Heart Disease dataset** (303 records, 13 attributes).
2. **Algorithm Dominance**: Ensemble machine learning techniques—specifically **Random Forest**—consistently outperform or match deep neural networks on tabular datasets under 500 records.
3. **Recall Priority**: Medical decision support literature prioritizes high **Recall (Sensitivity)** to minimize clinically dangerous False Negatives.
4. **Interactive Deployment**: The literature emphasizes transitioning theoretical models into user-friendly clinical applications (achieved via our **Streamlit web application**).
